INFOTAINMENT COSTS

TECHNICAL EXPENDITURE ANALYSIS

The cost of vehicle infotainment varies dramatically depending on whether you are looking at manufacturing/option pricing, aftermarket upgrades, or full dealership repairs. New cars provide options for an upgraded premium sound and infotainment package (e.g., Bose, Burmester, or expanded touchscreens) typically costing between $1,500 and $3,500 at the point of vehicle purchase. Replacing an older factory radio with a modern touchscreen unit supporting Apple CarPlay and Android Auto generally costs $300 to $1,200, factoring in parts (dash kits, wiring harnesses) and professional installation. And if a factory infotainment computer or integrated screen fails outside of warranty, dealership quotes frequently range from $800 to $4,000 due to complex head-unit integration and mandatory software/VIN programming.
